TextPort Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026

TextPort reads SMS messages from your Android device only after you explicitly grant consent and permission. SMS data is uploaded to your configured TextPort server account so you can view, search, filter, export, and manage your own message history.

What we collect:
- SMS sender address
- Message body
- Message timestamp
- Device identifier generated by the app

Controls provided in-app:
- Pause sync and resume sync
- Export data from your account
- Delete all account data and revoke sync access

We do not hide tracking, run silently without disclosure, or collect data unless sync is enabled by you.
